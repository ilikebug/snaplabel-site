Snaplabel: Label Maker

Print labels from your iPhone — free and unlimited, with no account and no subscription.

Snaplabel is a fast label maker for Bluetooth thermal label printers. Make a "Use by" label for the fridge in a few taps, print price tags and barcodes for your shop, or label every box in the garage. Printing never costs anything: no print limits, no watermarks, no sign-up. Everything works offline, and your labels stay on your iPhone.

Snaplabel Pro is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks extra creative tools and automation. There is no subscription. Labels you made with Pro stay printable for free.

No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Bluetooth is used only to connect to your label printer.
